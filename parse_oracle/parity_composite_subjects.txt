# Explicit parity predicates accept compound subjects and compose under binding.

id: parity-composite-subject-disjunction
context: nested
surface: Plus(a, b) is even or Plus(a, b) is odd
expect: Even(Plus(a, b)) or Odd(Plus(a, b))
why: Both alternatives explicitly repeat the same compound subject, which is parsed as a term argument to the corresponding parity predicate.

id: parity-reversed-order-disjunction
context: nested
surface: n is odd or n is even
expect: Odd(n) or Even(n)
why: The explicit alternatives may be stated in either parity order; their order in the formula must be preserved.

id: parity-composite-subject-in-conditional
context: nested
surface: if Plus(a, b) is even or Plus(a, b) is odd then P(a)
expect: (Even(Plus(a, b)) or Odd(Plus(a, b))) -> P(a)
why: The repeated compound subject is parsed within the antecedent disjunction before the conditional is assembled.

id: parity-disjunction-under-universal
context: nested
surface: forall n, n is odd or n is even
expect: forall n, (Odd(n) or Even(n))
why: The variable in both explicit alternatives remains bound by the surrounding universal quantifier.
