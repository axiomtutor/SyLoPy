# Explicit parity alternatives compose with ordinary connectives.

id: parity-explicit-even-or-odd
context: nested
surface: n is even or n is odd
expect: Even(n) or Odd(n)
why: Repeating the subject makes both parity alternatives explicit; each predicate lowers to its unary vocabulary symbol.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-as-implication-antecedent
context: nested
surface: if n is even or n is odd then P
expect: (Even(n) or Odd(n)) -> P
why: The explicit parity alternatives form the antecedent disjunction of the conditional.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-as-implication-consequent
context: nested
surface: if P then n is even or n is odd
expect: P -> (Even(n) or Odd(n))
why: The explicit parity alternatives form the consequent disjunction of the conditional.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-in-conjunction
context: nested
surface: P and (n is even or n is odd)
expect: P and (Even(n) or Odd(n))
why: The parenthesized parity disjunction composes as one operand of a conjunction.
