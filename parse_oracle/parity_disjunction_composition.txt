# Composition checks for the explicit even-or-odd disjunction.
# The repeated subject distinguishes this from the intentionally rejected
# shorthand "n is even or odd" in nested_quantifiers_and_number_phrases.txt.

id: parity-explicit-even-or-odd
context: nested
surface: n is even or n is odd
expect: Even(n) or Odd(n)
why: Repeating the subject makes the two alternatives explicit; each phrase lowers to its unary predicate.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-as-implication-antecedent
context: nested
surface: if n is even or n is odd then P
expect: (Even(n) or Odd(n)) -> P
why: The explicit parity alternatives compose as one disjunction in the conditional antecedent.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-as-implication-consequent
context: nested
surface: if P then n is even or n is odd
expect: P -> (Even(n) or Odd(n))
why: The explicit parity alternatives compose as one disjunction in the conditional consequent.

id: parity-explicit-disjunction-in-conjunction
context: nested
surface: P and (n is even or n is odd)
expect: P and (Even(n) or Odd(n))
why: Parenthesized explicit parity alternatives remain one operand of a conjunction.
