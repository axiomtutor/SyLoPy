# The shorthand parity disjunction stays rejected in compound formulas.

id: parity-ambiguous-shorthand-in-implication
context: nested
surface: if n is even or odd then P
expect: REJECT
why: The repeated subject is omitted in the second alternative, so the parity disjunction remains ambiguous inside a conditional antecedent.

id: parity-ambiguous-shorthand-in-conjunction
context: nested
surface: P and (n is even or odd)
expect: REJECT
why: Parenthesizing the shorthand as one conjunct must not give the incomplete second alternative an unambiguous reading.

id: parity-ambiguous-shorthand-under-negation
context: nested
surface: not (n is even or odd)
expect: REJECT
why: Negation changes only the outer connective and must not change how the inner shorthand parity phrase is interpreted.

id: parity-ambiguous-shorthand-under-universal-quantifier
context: nested
surface: forall n, n is even or odd
expect: REJECT
why: A universal quantifier must not make the ambiguous shorthand parity disjunction unambiguous.

id: parity-ambiguous-shorthand-under-existential-quantifier
context: nested
surface: exists n, n is even or odd
expect: REJECT
why: An existential quantifier must not make the ambiguous shorthand parity disjunction unambiguous.
