# Independent readings of natural-language logical syntax. The "expect"
# side uses only plain-logic forms; it does not copy the surface notation.

id: logic-natural-universal-conditional
context: line
surface: for all x, if P(x) then Q(x)
expect: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(x))
why: The natural-language quantifier and conditional translate to their first-order logical forms.

id: logic-natural-existential-conjunction
context: line
surface: there exists x such that P(x) and Q(x)
expect: exists x, (P(x) and Q(x))
why: One witness satisfies both conjuncts.

id: logic-natural-multiple-universal
context: line
surface: for all x, y such that R(x, y)
expect: forall x, (forall y, R(x, y))
why: The multi-variable "such that" form desugars to nested universal quantifiers.

id: logic-natural-multiple-existential
context: line
surface: there exists x, y such that R(x, y)
expect: exists x, (exists y, R(x, y))
why: The multi-variable existential form desugars to nested existential quantifiers.

id: logic-natural-conditional
context: nested
surface: if P then Q
expect: P -> Q
why: Natural-language conditional wording lowers to implication.

id: logic-implies-alias
context: nested
surface: P implies Q
expect: P -> Q
why: The word "implies" is an alias for the implication connective.

id: logic-biconditional-phrase
context: nested
surface: P if and only if Q
expect: P iff Q
why: The English biconditional phrase lowers to the logical biconditional.

id: logic-implication-consequent-precedence
context: nested
surface: P -> Q and R
expect: P -> (Q and R)
why: Conjunction groups inside the consequent of the implication.

id: logic-disjunction-conjunction-precedence
context: nested
surface: P or Q and R
expect: P or (Q and R)
why: Conjunction binds more tightly than disjunction.

id: logic-disjunction-implication-precedence
context: nested
surface: P or Q implies R
expect: (P or Q) -> R
why: Implication binds more loosely than disjunction, so the disjunction is its antecedent.

id: logic-negation-symbol
context: nested
surface: not P and Q
expect: (not P) and Q
why: Negation binds more tightly than conjunction, so only P is negated.

id: logic-not-equal-sugar
context: nested
surface: a =/= b
expect: not (a = b)
why: The inequality token lowers to negated equality.

id: logic-nested-quantifier-dependent-witness
context: nested
surface: for all x, there exists y such that R(x, y)
expect: forall x, exists y, R(x, y)
why: The existential quantifier remains inside the universal, allowing y to depend on x.

id: logic-nested-quantifier-shared-witness
context: nested
surface: there exists y such that for all x, R(x, y)
expect: exists y, forall x, R(x, y)
why: The existential quantifier scopes over the universal, requiring one witness to work for every x.

id: logic-free-variable-under-universal
context: nested
surface: for all x, if P(x) then Q(y)
expect: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(y))
why: The universal binds x but must leave y free.

id: logic-natural-existential-nested-in-conditional
context: nested
surface: if P(x) then there exists y such that R(x, y)
expect: P(x) -> exists y, R(x, y)
why: The existential is scoped inside the consequent of the conditional.

id: logic-exists-unique-lowering
context: line
surface: there exists a unique set Y such that P(Y)
expect: exists Y, (P(Y) and forall X, (P(X) -> X = Y))
why: Unique existence is expanded into existence plus a universal uniqueness condition.
