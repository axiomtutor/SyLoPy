# Plain-logic oracle cases for quantifier scope, free variables, and connective
# grouping. Expectations intentionally repeat the visible logical structure:
# these are parser regressions, not claims about validity.

id: chatgpt-prop-negated-conditional
context: nested
surface: not (P -> Q)
expect: not (P -> Q)
why: Negation scopes over the whole parenthesized conditional.

id: chatgpt-prop-conjunctive-antecedent
context: nested
surface: (P and Q) -> R
expect: (P and Q) -> R
why: Parentheses make the conjunction the full antecedent.

id: chatgpt-prop-disjunctive-consequent
context: nested
surface: P -> (Q or R)
expect: P -> (Q or R)
why: Parentheses make the disjunction the full consequent.

id: chatgpt-prop-biconditional-of-conditionals
context: nested
surface: (P -> Q) iff (Q -> P)
expect: (P -> Q) iff (Q -> P)
why: Both sides of the biconditional are parenthesized conditionals.

id: chatgpt-fl-universal-implication
context: line
surface: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(x))
expect: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(x))
why: The universal quantifier binds x throughout the conditional.

id: chatgpt-fl-existential-conjunction
context: line
surface: exists x, (P(x) and Q(x))
expect: exists x, (P(x) and Q(x))
why: A single witness must satisfy both conjuncts.

id: chatgpt-fl-universal-existential
context: nested
surface: forall x, exists y, R(x, y)
expect: forall x, exists y, R(x, y)
why: The existential is inside the universal, so y may depend on x.

id: chatgpt-fl-existential-universal
context: nested
surface: exists y, forall x, R(x, y)
expect: exists y, forall x, R(x, y)
why: The existential is outside the universal, so one y serves every x.

id: chatgpt-fl-free-variable-in-universal-body
context: nested
surface: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(y))
expect: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(y))
why: Quantifying x must not capture the distinct free variable y.

id: chatgpt-fl-free-variable-in-existential-body
context: nested
surface: exists y, (R(x, y) and P(x))
expect: exists y, (R(x, y) and P(x))
why: Quantifying y must not capture the distinct free variable x.

id: chatgpt-fl-negated-universal
context: nested
surface: not (forall x, P(x))
expect: not (forall x, P(x))
why: Negation scopes over the entire universally quantified formula.

id: chatgpt-fl-universal-negated-body
context: nested
surface: forall x, not P(x)
expect: forall x, not P(x)
why: The body of the universal quantifier is the negation of P(x).

id: chatgpt-fl-quantified-conditional
context: nested
surface: forall x, (P(x) -> exists y, R(x, y))
expect: forall x, (P(x) -> exists y, R(x, y))
why: The existential is scoped within the conditional's consequent and the universal's body.

id: chatgpt-fl-equality-under-universal
context: nested
surface: forall x, (x = a -> P(x))
expect: forall x, (x = a -> P(x))
why: The equality and predicate share the same universally bound x.

id: chatgpt-fl-function-term-equality
context: nested
surface: f(x) = f(y) -> x = y
expect: f(x) = f(y) -> x = y
why: Function terms are compared by equality inside a conditional; this is a reading test, not an assertion of validity.

id: chatgpt-fl-nary-predicate
context: nested
surface: forall x, R(x, a, b)
expect: forall x, R(x, a, b)
why: The predicate keeps all three arguments while x is bound.

id: chatgpt-fl-nested-existentials
context: nested
surface: exists x, (P(x) and exists y, (R(x, y) and Q(y)))
expect: exists x, (P(x) and exists y, (R(x, y) and Q(y)))
why: The inner witness y remains within the scope of the outer witness x.
