# Nested bounded-quantifier phrase variants.

id: j4-bounded-quantifier-disjunction-body
context: nested
surface: for every x in A, P(x) or there exists y in B such that R(x, y)
expect: forall x, (In(x, A) -> (P(x) or exists y, (In(y, B) and R(x, y))))
why: The universal restriction scopes over the whole disjunction.

id: j4-bounded-quantifiers-in-antecedent
context: nested
surface: if for all x in A, P(x), then there exists y in B such that Q(y)
expect: (forall x, (In(x, A) -> P(x))) -> exists y, (In(y, B) and Q(y))
why: Each bounded quantifier should parse as a complete formula before the outer implication.
