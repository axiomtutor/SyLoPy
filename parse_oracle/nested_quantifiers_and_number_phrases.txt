# More nested bounded-quantifier and number-theory readings.

id: j4-forall-exists-bounded-natural-language
context: nested
surface: for all x in A, there exists y in B such that R(x, y)
expect: forall x, (In(x, A) -> exists y, (In(y, B) and R(x, y)))
why: For each member x of A, a possibly x-dependent witness y must lie in B and satisfy R.

id: j4-exists-forall-bounded-natural-language
context: nested
surface: there exists x in A such that for all y in B, R(x, y)
expect: exists x, (In(x, A) and forall y, (In(y, B) -> R(x, y)))
why: One witness x in A must satisfy the condition for every member y of B.

id: j4-three-deep-bounded-quantifiers
context: nested
surface: forall x in A, exists y in B, forall z in C, R(x, y, z)
expect: forall x, (In(x, A) -> exists y, (In(y, B) and forall z, (In(z, C) -> R(x, y, z))))
why: The three restrictions map to implication, conjunction, and implication at their respective quantifier levels.

id: j4-bounded-quantifier-with-negative-body
context: nested
surface: forall x in A, there exists y in B such that not R(x, y)
expect: forall x, (In(x, A) -> exists y, (In(y, B) and not R(x, y)))
why: The negation applies to R inside the existential witness condition.

id: j4-exists-bounded-then-forall-unbounded
context: nested
surface: there exists x in A such that for all y, R(x, y)
expect: exists x, (In(x, A) and forall y, R(x, y))
why: The witness is restricted to A but the inner universal has no membership restriction.

id: j4-bounded-quantifiers-in-biconditional
context: nested
surface: (forall x in A, P(x)) iff (there exists y in B such that Q(y))
expect: (forall x, (In(x, A) -> P(x))) iff exists y, (In(y, B) and Q(y))
why: Parentheses isolate each quantifier phrase as one biconditional operand.

id: j4-divides-composed-dividend
context: nested
surface: a divides Plus(b, c) and c divides d
expect: (exists m, (Int(m) and Plus(b, c) = Times(a, m))) and (exists n, (Int(n) and d = Times(c, n)))
why: Each English divisibility claim expands independently in a conjunction.

id: j4-negated-composite-divisibility
context: nested
surface: not (a divides Plus(b, c))
expect: not (exists m, (Int(m) and Plus(b, c) = Times(a, m)))
why: Negation applies to the entire existential definition of divisibility.

id: j4-divisibility-under-existential
context: nested
surface: there exists a in A such that a divides n
expect: exists a, (In(a, A) and exists m, (Int(m) and n = Times(a, m)))
why: The bounded witness is also the divisor in the inner divisibility condition.

id: j4-parity-even-or-odd
context: nested
surface: n is even or odd
expect: REJECT
why: This abbreviation is intentionally ambiguous unless the language explicitly defines the alternative phrase for number predicates.

id: j4-malformed-divides-chain
context: line
surface: a divides b divides c
expect: REJECT
why: Divisibility is a binary predicate-like phrase and an unparenthesized chain does not specify a formula.

expect: REJECT
