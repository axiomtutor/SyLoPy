## Proof that 
### if a, b, c, are integers such that: a|b. a|c. 
### then a|(b+c).

1. Let a, b, c be integers such that: a|b. a|c. (Premise)
2. There exist m, n such that am = b and an = c. (Definition of Divisibility from 1)
3. b+c = am+an = a(m+n). (Substitution from 2; algebra)
4. m+n is an integer. (Closure of Integers from 2)
5. a|(b+c). (Definition of Divisibility from 3, 4)

## Proof that
### if a, b, c, are integers such that: a|b and b|c.
### then a|c.

1. Let a, b, c be integers such that: a|b and b|c. (Premise)
2. There exist m, n such that am = b and bn = c.  (Definition of Divisibility from 1)
3. amn = c. (Substitution from 1)
4. mn is an integer. (Closure of Integers from 2)
5. a|c. (Definition of Divisibility from 3,4)
