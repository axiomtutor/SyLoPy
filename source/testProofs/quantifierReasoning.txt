(*
A compact example of quantified reasoning:
instantiate two universal premises at an arbitrary object, apply modus ponens,
then generalize the result.
*)

# 1: Universal modus ponens
## Proof that
### if P and Q are predicates such that: forall x, (P(x) -> Q(x)). forall x, P(x).
### then forall x, Q(x).

1. Let P and Q be predicates. (Declare)
2. forall x, (P(x) -> Q(x)). (Premise)
3. forall x, P(x). (Premise)
4. forall x, Q(x). (Universal Generalization from subproof below)
begin subproof
 4.0. let c be arbitrary. (Arbitrary)
 4.1. P(c). (Universal Instantiation from 3)
 4.2. P(c) -> Q(c). (Universal Instantiation from 2)
 4.3. Q(c). (Modus Ponens from 4.1, 4.2)
end subproof
