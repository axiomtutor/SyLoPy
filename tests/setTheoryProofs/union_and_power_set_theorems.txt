# Theorems that cite the Axiom of union and the Axiom of power set for a
# particular set. Each cites the axiom already instantiated at the set it
# means (`exists Y, ...` for A, with no `forall X` in front), names the set it
# gets with "Let Y be such a set", and reasons about that set with
# `Set property`. Each proof stands alone, like the proofs in
# pairing_theorems.txt.
#
# Proofs 5 and 6 follow the "Unique pairing" proof in zfc_remaining_axioms.txt.
# They need neither WLOG nor Mutatis mutandis: the membership property of a
# union, or of a power set, has no symmetry between cases to exploit, and both
# inclusions are plain `Set property` steps.


# 1: A member of a member of A belongs to the union of A
## Proof that
### If B is in A and c is in B
### then there exists Y such that (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v))))) and In(c, Y).

1. Let A, B, c be any set. (Declaration)
2. In(B, A). (Premise)
3. In(c, B). (Premise)
4. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v)))). (Axiom of union)
5. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 4)
6. In(B, A) and In(c, B). (Conjunction Introduction from 2, 3)
7. Exists v, (In(v, A) and In(c, v)). (Existential Introduction from 6)
8. In(c, Y). (Set property from 5, 7)
9. (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v))))) and In(c, Y). (Conjunction Introduction from 5, 8)
10. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v))))) and In(c, Y)). (Existential Introduction from 9)


# 2: Every subset of A belongs to the power set of A
## Proof that
### If B is a subset of A
### then there exists Y such that (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(B, Y).

1. Let A, B be any set. (Declaration)
2. B is a subset of A. (Premise)
3. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A). (Axiom of power set)
4. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 3)
5. In(B, Y). (Set property from 4, 2)
6. (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(B, Y). (Conjunction Introduction from 4, 5)
7. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(B, Y)). (Existential Introduction from 6)


# 3: A set belongs to its own power set
## Proof that
### there exists Y such that (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(A, Y).

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. A is a subset of A. (Subset proof below)
 2.1. Let a in A. (Assumption for subset proof)
 2.2. a is in A. (Reiteration from 2.1)
3. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A). (Axiom of power set)
4. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 3)
5. In(A, Y). (Set property from 4, 2)
6. (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(A, Y). (Conjunction Introduction from 4, 5)
7. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(A, Y)). (Existential Introduction from 6)


# 4: The empty set belongs to the power set of any set
## Proof that
### there exists Y such that (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(the empty set, Y).

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. The empty set is a subset of A. (Subset proof below)
 2.1. Let a in the empty set. (Assumption for subset proof)
 2.2. a is not in the empty set. (Set property)
 2.3. a is in A. (Explosion from 2.1, 2.2)
3. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A). (Axiom of power set)
4. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 3)
5. In(the empty set, Y). (Set property from 4, 2)
6. (forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(the empty set, Y). (Conjunction Introduction from 4, 5)
7. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and In(the empty set, Y)). (Existential Introduction from 6)


# 5: Unique union
## Proof that
### If A is a set,
### then there exists a unique set Y such that for all u, In(u, Y) iff (exists v, In(v, A) and In(u, v)).

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v)))). (Axiom of union)
3. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 2)
4. Let X be any set. (Declaration)
5. If (forall z, (In(z, X) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(z, v))))) then X = Y. (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
 5.1. Forall z, (In(z, X) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(z, v)))). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
 5.2. X subseteq Y. (Subset proof below)
  5.2.1. Let x in X. (Assumption for subset proof)
  5.2.2. Exists v, (In(v, A) and In(x, v)). (Set property, 5.1, 5.2.1)
  5.2.3. In(x, Y). (Set property, 3, 5.2.2)
 5.3. Y subseteq X. (Subset proof below)
  5.3.1. Let y in Y. (Assumption for subset proof)
  5.3.2. Exists v, (In(v, A) and In(y, v)). (Set property, 3, 5.3.1)
  5.3.3. In(y, X). (Set property, 5.1, 5.3.2)
 5.4. X = Y. (Set equality, 5.2, 5.3)
6. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(u, v))))) and (forall X, ((forall z, (In(z, X) iff (exists v, (In(v, A) and In(z, v))))) -> X = Y))). (Uniqueness, 2, 5)


# 6: Unique power set
## Proof that
### If A is a set,
### then there exists a unique set Y such that for all u, In(u, Y) iff u subset A.

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. Exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A). (Axiom of power set)
3. Let Y be such a set. (Existence from 2)
4. Let X be any set. (Declaration)
5. If (forall z, (In(z, X) iff z subset A)) then X = Y. (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
 5.1. Forall z, (In(z, X) iff z subset A). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
 5.2. X subseteq Y. (Subset proof below)
  5.2.1. Let x in X. (Assumption for subset proof)
  5.2.2. x subset A. (Set property, 5.1, 5.2.1)
  5.2.3. In(x, Y). (Set property, 3, 5.2.2)
 5.3. Y subseteq X. (Subset proof below)
  5.3.1. Let y in Y. (Assumption for subset proof)
  5.3.2. y subset A. (Set property, 3, 5.3.1)
  5.3.3. In(y, X). (Set property, 5.1, 5.3.2)
 5.4. X = Y. (Set equality, 5.2, 5.3)
6. Exists Y, ((forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u subset A)) and (forall X, ((forall z, (In(z, X) iff z subset A)) -> X = Y))). (Uniqueness, 2, 5)
