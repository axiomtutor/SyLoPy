(*
J26 red-team cases for Mutatis mutandis stages 2 and 3.
These cases stress simultaneous term and symbol replacements, the shapes that
must remain fixed, and whether every changed outside citation has a visible
counterpart. Every invalid case has a valid control with the single relevant
condition repaired.

# 1: A self-containing term needs a visible counterpart
## Invalid: missing counterpart — replacing a by f(a) requires a visible line that says R(f(a)).

2. R(a). (Reiteration from 1)
3. R(f(a)). (Mutatis mutandis, 2)
