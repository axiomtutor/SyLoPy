# 1: A proposition is a zero-ary statement
## Proof that
### then P or not P.

1. Let P be a proposition. (Declaration)
2. P or not P. (Premise)
3. P or not P. (Reiteration from 2)

# 2: Propositional variables support proof by cases
## Proof that
### then R.

1. Let P, Q, R be propositional variables. (Declaration)
2. P or Q. (Premise)
3. If P then R. (Premise)
4. Q -> R. (Premise)
5. R. (Proof by Cases from 2, subproofs below)
begin subproof
 5.1.case1. P. (Case)
 5.2.case1. R. (Modus Ponens from 3, 5.1.case1)
end subproof
begin subproof
 5.1.case2. Q. (Case)
 5.2.case2. R. (Modus Ponens from 4, 5.1.case2)
end subproof
