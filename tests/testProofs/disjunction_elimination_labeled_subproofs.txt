# 1: Disjunction elimination with labeled explicit case blocks in an implicit subproof
## Proof that
### then S -> R

1. Let P, Q, R, S be closed formulas such that: P or Q. P -> R. Q -> R. (Premise)
2. S -> R. (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
 2.1. S. (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
 2.2. begin subproof
  2.2.1. P. (Assumption)
  2.2.2. R. (Modus Ponens from 1, 2.2.1)
 end subproof
 2.3. begin subproof
  2.3.1. Q. (Assumption)
  2.3.2. R. (Modus Ponens from 1, 2.3.1)
 end subproof
 2.4. R. (Disjunction Elimination from 1, 2.2, 2.3)
