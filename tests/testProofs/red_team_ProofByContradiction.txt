# 1: Proof by contradiction from an actual contradiction
## Proof that
### if P is a closed formula.
### then P or not P.

1. Let P be a closed formula. (Declaration)
2. P or not P. (Proof by Contradiction from subproof below)
begin subproof
 2.1. not (P or not P). (Assumption for Contradiction)
 2.2. not P and not not P. (De Morgans from 2.1)
end subproof

# 2: Proof by contradiction requires a contradictory subproof conclusion
