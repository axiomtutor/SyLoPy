# 1: Existential introduction from an instance
## Proof that
### then exists x, P(x).

1. Let a be an object and P be a predicate such that: P(a). (Premise)
2. exists x, P(x). (Existential Introduction from 1)

# 2: Existential introduction cannot use a universal formula as its instance
## Invalid: instance shape — a universal formula is not an instance of the existential body.
### then exists x, P(x).

1. forall x, P(x). (Premise)
2. exists x, P(x). (Existential Introduction from 1)

# 3: Existential introduction cannot switch predicate symbols
## Invalid: predicate mismatch — the cited formula does not instantiate the existential body's predicate.
### then exists x, P(x).

1. Let a be an object and Q be a predicate such that: Q(a). (Premise)
2. exists x, P(x). (Existential Introduction from 1)
