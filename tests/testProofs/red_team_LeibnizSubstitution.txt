# 1: Substitution changes a genuine occurrence of an equality endpoint
## Proof that
### then Q(b).

1. Let a and b be objects. (Declaration)
2. Let Q be a predicate. (Declaration)
3. a = b. (Premise)
4. Q(a). (Premise)
5. Q(b). (Substitution from 3, 4)
