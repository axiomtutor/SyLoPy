# 1: Transitive and irreflexive relations are asymmetric
## Proof that
### if R is transitive and irreflexive on X, then R(a,b) implies not R(b,a)
### then R(a, b) -> not R(b, a).

1. Let X be any set, R be a transitive, irreflexive relation on X, and a, b be in X. (Declaration)
2. R(a, b) -> not R(b, a). (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
begin subproof
 2.1. R(a, b). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
 2.2. not R(b, a). (Proof by Contradiction from subproof below)
 begin subproof
  2.2.1. not (not R(b, a)). (Assumption for Contradiction)
  2.2.2. R(b, a). (Double Negation from 2.2.1)
  2.2.3. R(a, a). (Relation Transitivity from 2.1, 2.2.2)
  2.2.4. not R(a, a). (Relation Irreflexivity from 1)
  2.2.5. R(a, a) and not R(a, a). (Conjunction Introduction from 2.2.3, 2.2.4)
 end subproof
end subproof
