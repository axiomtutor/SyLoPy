# 1: Asymmetric relations are irreflexive
## Proof that
### then not R(a, a).

1. Let X be any set, R be an asymmetric relation on X, and a be in X. (Declaration)
2. not R(a, a). (Proof by Contradiction from subproof below)
begin subproof
 2.1. R(a, a). (Assumption for contradiction)
 2.2. not R(a, a). (Relation Asymmetry from 2.1)
 2.3. R(a, a) and not R(a, a). (Conjunction Introduction from 2.1, 2.2)
end subproof
