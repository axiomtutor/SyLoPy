# 1
## Proof that
### reflexive relations give self-related elements
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be a reflexive relation on X, 
 a be in X. (Declaration)
2. R(a,a). (Relation Reflexivity from 1)

# 2
## Proof that
### symmetric relations reverse related pairs
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be a symmetric relation on X, 
 a, b be in X, 
 R(a,b). (Declaration)
2. R(b,a). (Relation Symmetry from 1)

# 3
## Proof that
### antisymmetric relations identify mutually related elements
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be an antisymmetric relation on X, 
 a, b be in X,  
 R(a,b) and R(b,a). (Declaration)
2. a = b. (Relation Antisymmetry from 1, 1)

# 4
## Proof that
### transitive relations compose matching pairs
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be a transitive relation on X, 
 a, b, c be in X, 
 R(a,b) and R(b,c). (Declaration)
2. R(a,c). (Relation Transitivity from 1, 1)

# 5
## Proof that
### equivalence relations have all three basic equivalence properties
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be an equivalence relation on X, 
 a, b, c be in X, 
 R(a,b) and R(b,c). (Declaration)
2. R(a,a). (Relation Reflexivity from 1)
3. R(b,a). (Relation Symmetry from 1)
4. R(a,c). (Relation Transitivity from 1, 1)

# 6
## Proof that
### partial orders have reflexivity antisymmetry and transitivity
1. Let X be any set, 
 R be a partial order on X, 
 a, b, c be in X, 
 R(a,b) and R(b,c). (Declaration)
2. R(a,a). (Relation Reflexivity from 1)
3. R(a,c). (Relation Transitivity from 1)
