

# 1
## Invalid proof that
### symmetry does not imply transitivity
1. Let X be any set, R be a symmetric relation on X, a, b, c be in X, and R(a,b) and R(b,c). (Declaration)
2. R(a,c). (Relation Transitivity from 1, 1)

# 2
## Invalid proof that
### transitivity does not reverse a pair
1. Let X be any set, R be a transitive relation on X, a, b be in X, and R(a,b). (Declaration)
2. R(b,a). (Relation Symmetry from 1)

# 3
## Invalid proof that
### antisymmetry requires both directions
1. Let X be any set, R be an antisymmetric relation on X, a, b be in X. (Declaration)
2. R(a,b). (Premise)
3. a = b. (Relation Antisymmetry from 2, 2)

# 4
## Invalid proof that
### transitivity requires the middle terms to match
1. Let X be any set, R be a transitive relation on X, a, b, c, d be in X, and R(a,b) and R(c,d). (Declaration)
2. R(a,d). (Relation Transitivity from 1, 1)

# 5
## Invalid proof that
### a relation property belongs to the declared relation only
1. Let X be any set, R be a transitive relation on X, S be a relation on X, a, b, c be in X, and S(a,b) and S(b,c). (Declaration)
2. S(a,c). (Relation Transitivity from 1, 1)
