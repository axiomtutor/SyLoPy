# 1: Negation preserves integerhood
## Proof that
### if a is an integer, then its additive inverse is an integer

1. Let a be an object. (Declaration)
2. Int(a). (Premise)
3. forall x, (Int(x) -> Int(Neg(x))). (Axiom)
4. Int(a) -> Int(Neg(a)). (Universal Instantiation from 3)
5. Int(Neg(a)). (Modus Ponens from 4, 2)
