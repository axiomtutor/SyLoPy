# 1: The negation of a sum of integers is an integer
## Proof that
### Int(a) and Int(b) imply Int(Neg(Plus(a,b)))

1. Let a, b be objects. (Declaration)
2. Int(a). (Premise)
3. Int(b). (Premise)
4. forall x, forall y, ((Int(x) and Int(y)) -> Int(Plus(x, y))). (Axiom)
5. forall y, ((Int(a) and Int(y)) -> Int(Plus(a, y))). (Universal Instantiation from 4)
6. (Int(a) and Int(b)) -> Int(Plus(a, b)). (Universal Instantiation from 5)
7. Int(a) and Int(b). (Conjunction Introduction from 2, 3)
8. Int(Plus(a, b)). (Modus Ponens from 6, 7)
9. forall x, (Int(x) -> Int(Neg(x))). (Axiom)
10. Int(Plus(a, b)) -> Int(Neg(Plus(a, b))). (Universal Instantiation from 9)
11. Int(Neg(Plus(a, b))). (Modus Ponens from 10, 8)
