# 1
## Proof that
### if m is an integer.
### then Zero/Zero = m.

1. Let m be an integer. (Declaration)
2. Int(m). (Premise)
3. Zero = Times(Zero, m). (Premise)
4. Zero/Zero = m. (Quotient Uniqueness from 2, 3)
