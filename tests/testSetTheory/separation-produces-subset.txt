# 1: Separation yields a subset of the original set
## Proof that
### if membership in S implies membership in X
### then S subseteq X.

1. Let S, X be any sets. (Declaration)
2. Let P be a predicate such that: forall u, (In(u, S) iff (In(u, X) and P(u))). (Premise)
3. S subseteq X. (Subset proof below)
 begin subproof
  3.1. Let u in S. (Assumption for subset proof)
  3.2. In(u, S) iff (In(u, X) and P(u)). (Universal Instantiation from 2)
  3.3. In(u, S) -> (In(u, X) and P(u)). (Biconditional Elimination from 3.2)
  3.4. In(u, X) and P(u). (Modus Ponens from 3.3, 3.1)
  3.5. In(u, X). (Conjunction Elimination from 3.4)
 end subproof
