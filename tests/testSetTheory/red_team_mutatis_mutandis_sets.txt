(*
J26 red-team cases for rules whose formulas are specific to set membership.
Every invalid axiom replay differs from the valid control only by replacing In
with R in the one target axiom formula. Each target also exercises a -> f(a).
*)

# 1: The four set axiom rules allow a term replacement while keeping In fixed
## Proof that
### then (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = f(a) or u = b)) and exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, f(a)) and In(u, v))))) and (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, f(a)))) and exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, f(a)) and P(u)))).

1. Let a and b be any sets, f be a function, and P and R be predicates. (Declaration)
2. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = a or u = b)). (Axiom of pairing)
3. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, a) and In(u, v)))). (Axiom of union)
4. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, a))). (Axiom of power set)
5. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, a) and P(u))). (Separation)
6. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = f(a) or u = b)). (Mutatis mutandis, 2)
7. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, f(a)) and In(u, v)))). (Mutatis mutandis, 3)
8. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, f(a)))). (Mutatis mutandis, 4)
9. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, f(a)) and P(u))). (Mutatis mutandis, 5)
10. (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = f(a) or u = b))) and (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, f(a)) and In(u, v))))). (Conjunction Introduction from 6, 7)
11. (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, f(a))))) and (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, f(a)) and P(u)))). (Conjunction Introduction from 8, 9)
12. ((exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = f(a) or u = b))) and (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, f(a)) and In(u, v)))))) and ((exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, f(a))))) and (exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, f(a)) and P(u))))). (Conjunction Introduction from 10, 11)
