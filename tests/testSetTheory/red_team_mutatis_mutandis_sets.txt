(*
J26 red-team cases for membership-specific set rules. Case 1 is the shared
valid control: closed-term substitution in all four set axioms and a set-name
replacement in SetProperty. Cases 2-5 change only the membership symbol in
one repeated axiom formula, which the corresponding axiom-specific rule rejects.
*)

# 1: The set axiom rules allow term substitution while preserving membership
## Proof that
### then In(a, f(Y)).

1. Let a and b be any sets, f be a function, and P and R be predicates. (Declaration)
2. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = a or u = b)). (Axiom of pairing)
3. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, a) and In(u, v)))). (Axiom of union)
4. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, a))). (Axiom of power set)
5. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, a) and P(u))). (Separation)
6. forall u, (In(u, Y) iff u = a). (Premise)
7. forall u, (In(u, f(Y)) iff u = a). (Premise)
8. a = a. (Reflexivity)
9. In(a, Y). (Set property from 6, 8)
10. In(a, f(Y)). (Mutatis mutandis, 9)
11. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = f(a) or u = b)). (Mutatis mutandis, 2)
12. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (exists v, (In(v, f(a)) and In(u, v)))). (Mutatis mutandis, 3)
13. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff forall w, (In(w, u) -> In(w, f(a)))). (Mutatis mutandis, 4)
14. exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (In(u, f(a)) and P(u))). (Mutatis mutandis, 5)
