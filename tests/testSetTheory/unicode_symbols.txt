# 1: Bounded universal instantiation using symbolic notation
## Proof that
### P(a).

1. Let A be any set, P be a unary predicate, a be an object. (Declaration)
2. ∀x ∈ A, P(x). (Premise)
3. a ∈ A. (Premise)
4. a ∈ A → P(a). (Universal Instantiation from 2)
5. P(a). (Modus Ponens from 3, 4)

# 2: A subset of a union
## Proof that
### A ⊆ A ∪ B.

1. Let A, B be any sets. (Declaration)
2. A ⊆ A ∪ B. (Premise)
3. A ⊆ A ∪ B. (Reiteration from 2)

# 3: De Morgan's law in symbolic notation
## Proof that
### ¬P ∨ ¬Q.

1. Let P, Q be propositions. (Declaration)
2. ¬(P ∧ Q). (Premise)
3. ¬P ∨ ¬Q. (De Morgans from 2)

# 4: Invalid: nonmembership does not imply membership
## Invalid: the premise states nonmembership, not membership
### a ∈ A.

1. Let A be any set, a be an object. (Declaration)
2. a ∉ A. (Premise)
3. a ∈ A. (Reiteration from 2)

# 5: Invalid: inequality does not imply equality
## Invalid: the premise is negated equality
### a = b.

1. Let a, b be objects. (Declaration)
2. a ≠ b. (Premise)
3. a = b. (Reiteration from 2)
