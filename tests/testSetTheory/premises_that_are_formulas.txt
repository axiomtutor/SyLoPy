(* 
Regression proofs for J44. A whole premise clause that a base-logic or
set-theory formula parser recognizes is a formula, not a declaration.
*)
# 1: The equality wording
## Proof that
### then A = B.

1. Let A, B be sets. (Declaration)
2. A and B are equal. (Premise)
3. A = B. (Reiteration from 2)

# 2: The not-equal wording
## Proof that
### then not (A = B).

1. Let A, B be sets. (Declaration)
2. A and B are not equal. (Premise)
3. not (A = B). (Reiteration from 2)

## Proof that
### then A = B.

2. A = B. (Reiteration from 1)
