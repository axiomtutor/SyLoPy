(*
J43 red-team proofs for the definition rules. Each invalid case names the
specific mismatch, capture, or citation-shape error it is intended to expose.
*)

# 1: The union definition with a vacuous and reordered universal closure
## Proof that
### then forall z, forall B, forall u, forall A, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))).

1. Let A, B be any set. (Declaration)
2. forall z, forall B, forall u, forall A, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))). (Definition of union)

# 2: The power-set definition through subset notation
## Proof that
### then forall u, (In(u, PowerSet(A)) iff u subseteq A).

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. forall u, (In(u, PowerSet(A)) iff u subseteq A). (Definition of power set)

# 3: A definition-derived disjunction supports proof by cases
## Proof that
### then P(x).

1. Let A, B be any set, x be any object, and P be a unary predicate. (Declaration)
2. In(x, Union(A, B)). (Premise)
3. In(x, A) -> P(x). (Premise)
4. In(x, B) -> P(x). (Premise)
5. forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))). (Definition of union)
6. In(x, A) or In(x, B). (Set property from 5, 2)
7. P(x). (Proof by Cases from 6, subproofs below)
begin subproof
 7.1.case1. In(x, A). (Case)
 7.2.case1. P(x). (Modus Ponens from 3, 7.1.case1)
end subproof
begin subproof
 7.1.case2. In(x, B). (Case)
 7.2.case2. P(x). (Modus Ponens from 4, 7.1.case2)
end subproof

# 4: Invalid: an extra conjunct is not part of the definition
## Invalid: extra conjunct — the cited line must be the defining biconditional itself.
### then forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))) and a = a.

1. Let A, B be any set. (Declaration)
2. (forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B)))) and a = a. (Definition of union)

# 5: Invalid: the subset-clause quantifier captures the element
## Invalid: variable capture — the inner forall w binds the w that is meant to be the element of the power set.
### then forall w, (In(w, PowerSet(A)) iff (forall w, (In(w, w) -> In(w, A)))).

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. forall w, (In(w, PowerSet(A)) iff (forall w, (In(w, w) -> In(w, A)))). (Definition of power set)

# 6: Invalid: a definition cannot be cited with a line reference
## Invalid: cited line — a definition is a zero-premise rule and takes no cited lines.
### then forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))).

1. Let A, B be any set. (Declaration)
2. forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))). (Definition of union)
3. forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))). (Definition of union from 2)

# 7: Invalid: a defining rule is not a rule for an atom
## Invalid: not a sentence — the cited line is a membership atom, not the definition's biconditional sentence.
### then In(x, Union(A, B)).

1. Let A, B be any set, and x be any object. (Declaration)
2. In(x, Union(A, B)). (Definition of union)

# 8: Invalid: the intersection sentence is not the union definition
## Invalid: wrong operation — the definition of union does not define the members of intersection.
### then forall u, (In(u, Intersection(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) and In(u, B))).

1. Let A, B be any set. (Declaration)
2. forall u, (In(u, Intersection(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) and In(u, B))). (Definition of union)

# 9: A definition citation can be repeated with both set names exchanged
## Proof that
### then In(x, Union(B, A)) iff (In(x, B) or In(x, A)).

1. Let A, B be any set, and x be any object. (Declaration)
2. In(x, Union(A, B)) iff (In(x, A) or In(x, B)). (Definition of union)
3. In(x, Union(B, A)) iff (In(x, B) or In(x, A)). (Mutatis mutandis, 2)

# 10: WLOG replay keeps a definition citation in the repeated region
## Proof that
### then In(y, A) or In(y, B).

1. Let A, B be any set, and a, x, y be any object. (Declaration)
2. x is in A union B. (Premise)
3. y is in A union B. (Premise)
4. a = x or a = y. (Premise)
5. a = x. (WLOG from 4)
6. forall u, (In(u, Union(A, B)) iff (In(u, A) or In(u, B))). (Definition of union)
7. In(x, A) or In(x, B). (Set property from 6, 2)
