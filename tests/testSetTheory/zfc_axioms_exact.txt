(*
The axioms of extensionality, foundation (also called regularity) and choice are
cited by name, and each citation is accepted for one sentence: the axiom, up to
the names of its bound variables. They used to be accepted for any sentence of
their rough shape. Each invalid case below was accepted that way, or restates an
axiom with `(Axiom)`, and set theory takes no formula as an axiom that way.

Foundation's form with `not (x = EmptySet)` as its antecedent is not cited by
name (the owner's decision on PR #160): it is a theorem, and proof 7 refuses it
as an axiom while proof 17 derives it from the axiom of foundation, the axiom of
extensionality and the property of the empty set. Choice is Zermelo's form: a
set of nonempty, pairwise disjoint sets has a set that meets each of them in
exactly one element.
*)

# 1: Extensionality
## Proof that
### the axiom of extensionality says that sets with the same members are equal,
### then forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, x) iff In(z, y))) -> x = y).

1. forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, x) iff In(z, y))) -> x = y). (Axiom of extensionality)

# 2: Extensionality, with other names for its bound variables
## Proof that
### the names of bound variables do not matter,
### then forall P, forall Q, ((forall r, (In(r, P) iff In(r, Q))) -> P = Q).

1. forall P, forall Q, ((forall r, (In(r, P) iff In(r, Q))) -> P = Q). (Axiom of extensionality)

# 3: Extensionality, with the biconditional the other way round
## Proof that
### then forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, y) iff In(z, x))) -> x = y).

1. forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, y) iff In(z, x))) -> x = y). (Axiom of extensionality)

# 5: Foundation
## Proof that
### the axiom of foundation says that a set with a member has a member that shares none with it,
### then forall x, ((exists y, In(y, x)) -> exists y, (In(y, x) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, x))))).

1. forall x, ((exists y, In(y, x)) -> exists y, (In(y, x) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, x))))). (Axiom of foundation)

# 6: Regularity, the same axiom under its other name

# 8: Choice

1. forall X, (((forall A, (In(A, X) -> exists a, In(a, A))) and (forall A, forall B, ((In(A, X) and In(B, X) and not (A = B)) -> not (exists c, (In(c, A) and In(c, B)))))) -> exists C, forall A, (In(A, X) -> exists a, (In(a, A) and In(a, C) and forall b, ((In(b, A) and In(b, C)) -> b = a)))). # 9: A false sentence cited as foundation
## Invalid: not the axiom — the shape of foundation (a universal over an implication that ends in an existential) is not the axiom.
### then exists y, not (y = y).

1. forall x, (Finite(EmptySet) -> exists y, not (y = y)). (Axiom of foundation)
2. Finite(EmptySet). (Empty set finite)
3. Finite(EmptySet) -> exists y, not (y = y). (Universal Instantiation from 1)
4. exists y, not (y = y). (Modus Ponens from 2, 3)

# 10: Using the axiom of extensionality
## Proof that
### sets with the same members are equal,
### then X = Y.

1. Let X, Y be any sets. (Declaration)
2. forall z, (In(z, X) iff In(z, Y)). (Premise)
3. forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, x) iff In(z, y))) -> x = y). (Axiom of extensionality)
4. forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, X) iff In(z, y))) -> X = y). (Universal Instantiation from 3)
5. (forall z, (In(z, X) iff In(z, Y))) -> X = Y. (Universal Instantiation from 4)
6. X = Y. (Modus Ponens from 2, 5)

# 17: Foundation for a set that is not the empty set, derived from the axiom
## Proof that
### a set that is not the empty set has a member that shares none with it,
### then forall x, (not (x = EmptySet) -> exists y, (In(y, x) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, x))))).

1. forall x, ((exists y, In(y, x)) -> exists y, (In(y, x) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, x))))). (Axiom of foundation)
2. forall x, forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, x) iff In(z, y))) -> x = y). (Axiom of extensionality)
3. forall x, (not (x = EmptySet) -> exists y, (In(y, x) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, x))))). (Universal Generalization from subproof below)
begin subproof
 3.0. let s be arbitrary. (Arbitrary)
 3.1. not (s = EmptySet) -> exists y, (In(y, s) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, s)))). (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
 begin subproof
  3.1.1. not (s = EmptySet). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
  3.1.2. exists y, In(y, s). (Proof by Contradiction from subproof below)
  begin subproof
   3.1.2.1. not (exists y, In(y, s)). (Assumption for Contradiction)
   3.1.2.2. forall z, (In(z, s) iff In(z, EmptySet)). (Universal Generalization from subproof below)
   begin subproof
    3.1.2.2.0. let w be arbitrary. (Arbitrary)
    3.1.2.2.1. In(w, s) -> In(w, EmptySet). (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
    begin subproof
     3.1.2.2.1.1. In(w, s). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
     3.1.2.2.1.2. exists y, In(y, s). (Existential Introduction from 3.1.2.2.1.1)
     3.1.2.2.1.3. In(w, EmptySet). (Explosion from 3.1.2.2.1.2, 3.1.2.1)
    end subproof
    3.1.2.2.2. In(w, EmptySet) -> In(w, s). (Conditional Introduction from subproof below)
    begin subproof
     3.1.2.2.2.1. In(w, EmptySet). (Assumption for Conditional Introduction)
     3.1.2.2.2.2. not In(w, EmptySet). (Set property)
     3.1.2.2.2.3. In(w, s). (Explosion from 3.1.2.2.2.1, 3.1.2.2.2.2)
    end subproof
    3.1.2.2.3. In(w, s) iff In(w, EmptySet). (Biconditional Introduction from 3.1.2.2.1, 3.1.2.2.2)
   end subproof
   3.1.2.3. forall y, ((forall z, (In(z, s) iff In(z, y))) -> s = y). (Universal Instantiation from 2)
   3.1.2.4. (forall z, (In(z, s) iff In(z, EmptySet))) -> s = EmptySet. (Universal Instantiation from 3.1.2.3)
   3.1.2.5. s = EmptySet. (Modus Ponens from 3.1.2.2, 3.1.2.4)
   3.1.2.6. (s = EmptySet) and not (s = EmptySet). (Conjunction Introduction from 3.1.2.5, 3.1.1)
  end subproof
  3.1.3. (exists y, In(y, s)) -> exists y, (In(y, s) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, s)))). (Universal Instantiation from 1)
  3.1.4. exists y, (In(y, s) and not (exists z, (In(z, y) and In(z, s)))). (Modus Ponens from 3.1.2, 3.1.3)
 end subproof
end subproof
