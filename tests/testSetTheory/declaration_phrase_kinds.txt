# 1: A strict order is a relation
## Proof that
### then R(a, b).

1. Let X be any set, R be a strict order on X, a, b be in X. (Declaration)
2. R(a, b). (Premise)

# 2: A bijection may be declared by its domain and codomain
## Proof that
### then f(a) = f(a).

1. Let A, B be any set, f be a bijection from A to B, a be in A. (Declaration)
2. f(a) = f(a). (Reflexivity)
