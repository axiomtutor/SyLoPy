# 6: The canonical subset proof is accepted
## Proof that
### then A subseteq A.

1. Let A be any set. (Declaration)
2. A subseteq A. (Subset proof below)
begin subproof
  2.1. Let x in A. (Assumption for subset proof)
  2.2. In(x, A). (Reiteration from 2.1)
end subproof
