# 1: Pairing, cited by name
## Proof that
### the axiom of pairing says that any a and b have a pair,
### then forall a, forall b, exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = a or u = b)).

1. forall a, forall b, exists Y, forall u, (In(u, Y) iff (u = a or u = b)). (Axiom of pairing)

# 3: Union, cited by name
